# Zombie couple video guide

Practical notes for making the "zombie love story" vertical clip (one partner has turned, the other cannot shoot, cut to a happy memory) from **two separate solo photos** — no photo of the two of you together needed.

The model is rarely the problem. Most bad results come from two input photos that do not match each other: different shot size, camera height, lens distance or light. This repo is the checklist for matching them, plus export and posting numbers for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## The one-minute version

| Check | Photo 1 (survivor) | Photo 2 (the one who turns) |
|---|---|---|
| People per photo | one | one |
| Shot size | head and shoulders | head and shoulders, face about the same size |
| Face pixels | ≥ 300 px chin to hairline | ≥ 300 px chin to hairline |
| Camera height | eye level | eye level |
| Camera distance | 1.5–3 m (or both selfies) | same as photo 1 |
| Face angle | ≤ 30° from the camera | ≤ 30° from the camera |
| Light | soft, from the front or a side window | same side and softness |
| Colour | skin tones similar after correction | skin tones similar after correction |
| Expression | neutral or small closed smile | neutral or small closed smile |
| Over the face | nothing (no sunglasses, mask, filter, hand) | nothing |

Backgrounds do not need to match — the scene replaces them. Clothes are usually kept.

## Troubleshooting table

| What you see in the clip | Most likely cause in the input | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Partner's face does not look like them | Face small in its source (wide shot, group crop) | Re-crop to head-and-shoulders; use a sharper photo |
| Both faces drift toward one "average" face | Very different angle and light in the two photos | Match eye level and colour first |
| Mouth or teeth melt during the snarl | Wide open-mouth laugh in the source | Use a closed-mouth photo |
| Glasses morph or vanish between frames | Reflective lenses, thick frames | Use a photo without glasses |
| Waxy, plastic skin | Beauty filter, or forehead/cheeks clipped to white | Use an unfiltered, correctly exposed photo |
| Nose looks bigger than in real life | Arm's-length selfie (0.4–0.6 m, wide lens) | Photo taken from 1.5 m+ or with the 2× lens |
| Body proportions wrong in the leap | One input is a face-only crop | Include shoulders in both inputs |
| One face orange, one blue | ~2700 K bulb photo next to ~7000 K shade photo | Correct warmth on one photo before upload |
| Pet looks like a different breed | Pet shot from above / far away / under warm bulbs | Daylight photo at the pet's eye level, head filling the frame |

## Tool

These notes come from making clips with [Zombie Couple AI](https://zombiecoupleai.org), a browser generator that turns two photos into a 15-second, 720p, 9:16 zombie couple clip. Its own walkthrough for the two-photo case is [How to make a zombie couple video with two separate photos](https://zombiecoupleai.org/guides/zombie-couple-video-with-two-separate-photos); the dog/cat variant is [Zombie pet video](https://zombiecoupleai.org/effects/zombie-pet-video); prices are on [Pricing](https://zombiecoupleai.org/pricing).

The checks themselves are tool-agnostic: any image-to-video model gives better faces when the two inputs already agree on camera height, distance and light.
